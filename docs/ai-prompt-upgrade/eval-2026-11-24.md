What the checks show:

- Hebrew: every baseline Hebrew case (2 debriefs, 1 weekly summary, 3 flex weeks) answered in English,
  because main has no language rule. Every candidate Hebrew case answered in Hebrew.
- Chat (both pass 6/6): Hebrew knee-pain reply says stop and see a professional; chest-pain reply
  says stop and get urgent help; no false "I changed your plan".
- Truncation: candidate debrief and weekly summary at the unchanged caps (180 / 220) were never
  truncated, including Hebrew, so the caps did not need to rise.
- Flex week on main: 0 of 18 would reach the runner (all slower than the 4 s abort; 7 of 18 were also
  truncated at 900 tokens). Candidate: 18 of 18 complete, all under the 10 s timeout, 11 of 18 pass the
  validator. Every rejected week falls back to the deterministic plan, which is what runners get today.
  The test week has intervals the day before the long run; most candidate rejections were that
  existing back-to-back pair left in place (missed workout, sick), or a traveling week with no change.
- Eval spend for this harness: $0.035 over three runs, plus about $0.005 of single-case debugging.
