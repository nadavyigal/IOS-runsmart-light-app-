RunSmart 1.2.5 (43): Apple Watch release candidate

This is the build we plan to submit to the App Store. It has the same Watch feature you tested in build 41, plus fixes to how a Watch run completes your plan after a Health sync. Please repeat the Watch check on this build.

Install on the iPhone that is paired with your Watch. No separate RunSmart Watch app is needed. Use your existing RunSmart account.

1. In Plan, open an incomplete interval session and tap Send to Apple Watch. In Apple's Workout app on the Watch, check that the session shows the right warmup, repeats, recoveries and cooldown.
2. Start, finish and save the workout on the Watch.
3. Back on the iPhone, open RunSmart, go to Profile, tap the HealthKit tile under Connected, and tap Sync Now.
4. Check that you see one run with the right distance and time, and that the session is marked done in Plan.
5. Sync again and reopen RunSmart. There should be no second run and no second completion.

If a step fails, send TestFlight feedback with the step number, what you expected, what you saw, and your iPhone and Watch models.
