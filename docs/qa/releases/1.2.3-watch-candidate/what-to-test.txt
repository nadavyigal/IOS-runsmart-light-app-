RunSmart Apple Watch candidate — 1.2.3 (41)

This candidate combines the Watch feature tested in build 38 with current iPhone sharing, saved-route benchmarks and plan/analytics fixes. It also fixes deferred plan completion after a temporary Health association lookup failure.

Install on the iPhone already paired with your Watch. No separate RunSmart Watch app is needed. Use your existing RunSmart account.

1. Send one supported incomplete planned interval session to Apple Watch. In Apple's Workout app, check one scheduled item with the correct warmup, repetitions, recoveries/cooldown and any configured pace alerts.
2. Start, finish and save the workout on Watch with the phone left behind.
3. Reconnect and let Apple Health sync. In RunSmart, choose Connections > Apple Health > Sync Now; allow workout reads (route and heart-rate reads are optional).
4. Confirm exactly one accurate run and one intended planned-workout completion. Compare distance and moving time with Apple Workout.
5. Sync again and reopen RunSmart: no duplicate run or completion.

If possible, retry after a temporary network interruption and check an edited/canceled session cannot complete a replacement session. Report the failed step, expected/actual behavior, build number, and iPhone/Watch models and OS versions. Use TestFlight feedback for screenshots; precise routes and personal health details are not needed.

Known limits: manual/foreground Health sync, possible Watch delivery delay, latest 100 workouts/180 days, no Health-deletion propagation, no live custom RunSmart coaching inside Apple's Workout app. Sessions with incomplete running targets cannot be exported. Garmin and Strava remain disabled.
