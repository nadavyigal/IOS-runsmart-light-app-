Record-a-run testing build — Releases A, B and C combined.

Please test:
1. Start a run, including Cancel GPS search and Start anyway after 20 seconds.
2. Record with the phone locked and Spotify playing; check speech cues and restored music volume.
3. Force-quit while recording and while paused, reopen, and restore the unfinished run.
4. Stop at a traffic light, then resume running. Check auto-pause, moving time, distance and splits.
5. Start a planned interval workout and check each step card and spoken cue.
6. Finish using the hold gesture. Review the summary, title, notes and splits.
7. Check the completed workout route, pauses and estimated calories in Apple Health.
8. Share with the route map off, then on. Check the map excludes the start/finish area.

Strava is deferred and hidden. Title and notes are stored on this device only. Calorie values are estimates. A Health save skipped after a failed duplicate check currently has no automatic retry.
